Furure goals

1. Where would I like to work in 1 or 2 years? What would my dream job look like?

In one or two years, I would like to work in a position related to data science, bioinformatics or computational biology. Ideally, I would work at a research institute, hospital or biotechnology company where I can combine my background in biology with my interest in data analysis.

My dream job would involve analysing biological and medical datasets, identifying patterns and interpreting results to answer scientific questions. I am particularly interested in gene expression analysis and single-cell RNA sequencing. I would enjoy working with large datasets and using programming languages such as R and Python to gain new insights from biological data.

2. What is my current position regarding the skills needed for this job? Which skills do I already have?

I am currently a third-year student of Biology and Medical Laboratory Research at Hogeschool Utrecht. During my studies, I have discovered that my main interest lies in data science rather than laboratory work.

I already have experience with several relevant skills: - R programming: using R for data processing, analysis and visualisation. - Gene expression analysis: performing differential gene expression analysis and interpreting results. - Single-cell RNA sequencing: independently analysing datasets using the Seurat package. - Statistical analysis: applying statistical methods to biological datasets. - Data visualisation: creating graphs and visualisations to communicate

results. - Microsoft Office: working with Excel and Word. Additionally, my work experience has helped me develop communication, teamwork, problem-solving and independent working skills.

3. Which skills would I still need or like to learn?

To prepare myself for a future career in data science or bioinformatics, I would like to develop several additional skills: - Python programming: expanding my programming knowledge beyond R. - Machine learning: learning how to apply machine learning techniques to

biological datasets. - Advanced R programming: writing more efficient and reproducible code. - Advanced bioinformatics: gaining more experience with complex biological

datasets and analysis methods. - Statistical modelling: improving my knowledge of statistical methods. - Data visualisation: developing more advanced ways to present complex data.

I would particularly like to learn Python and machine learning because these skills could help me analyse larger and more complex datasets in the future.
