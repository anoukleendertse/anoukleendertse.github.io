About me

My name is Anouk Leendertse, and I am a third-year student of Biology and Medical Laboratory Research at the University of Applied Sciences Utrecht (Hogeschool Utrecht).

Although my educational background is mainly in lab-biology, my main interest lies in data science and data analysis. I enjoy working with data, exploring patterns and using programming tools such as R to analyse and interpret information. I have done some work already in R.

I would like to work in a position where I can combine my background in biology with data science and healthcare. I am interested in opportunities within bioinformatics, genomics or healthcare data science, for example at a research institute, university, hospital or biotechnology company. A management function is also something I am interested in and would like to further explore. I would enjoy working with large datasets and using programming languages such as R and Python to gain new insights into biological processes and human health.

Alongside my studies, I have gained several years of work experience in retail. These experiences have helped me develop strong communication skills, a sense of responsibility and the ability to work effectively in a team.

I am eager to further develop my skills in data science and gain practical experience in this field.
