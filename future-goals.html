Future goals

1. Where would I like to work in 1 or 2 years? What would my dream job look like?

In one or two years, I would like to work in a position where I can combine my background in biology with data science and healthcare. I am interested in opportunities within bioinformatics, genomics or healthcare data science, for example at a research institute, university, hospital or biotechnology company.

My dream job would involve analysing biological and medical datasets, identifying patterns and interpreting results to answer scientific questions. I am also interested in gene expression analysis and machine learning, as well as analysing medical data to discover patterns and relationships related to the development and progression of diseases. I would enjoy working with large datasets and using programming languages such as R and Python to gain new insights into biological processes and human health.

2. What is my current position regarding the skills needed for this job? Which skills do I already have?

I am currently a third-year student of Biology and Medical Laboratory Research at Hogeschool Utrecht. During my studies, I have discovered that my main interest lies in data science and data analysis rather than laboratory work.

I have already gained experience with several skills that are relevant to my future career:

  • R programming: Using R for data processing, analysis and visualisation.
  • Gene expression analysis: Performing differential gene expression analysis and interpreting the results.
  • Single-cell RNA sequencing: Independently analysing datasets using the Seurat package in R.
  • Statistical analysis: Applying statistical methods to biological datasets.
  • Data visualisation: Creating graphs and visualisations to present and communicate results.
  • Microsoft Office: Working with programs such as Excel, Word and Teams.

In addition to my technical skills, my work experience has helped me develop communication, teamwork, problem-solving and independent working skills. Although I already have a good foundation in biological data analysis (R), I would like to further develop my programming skills, particularly in Python, as well as gain more experience with machine learning and larger biological and medical datasets.

3. Which skills would I still need or like to learn?

To prepare myself for a future career in data science or bioinformatics, I would like to develop several additional skills:

  • Python programming: expanding my programming knowledge beyond R.

  • Machine learning: learning how to apply machine learning techniques to biological datasets.

  • Advanced R programming: writing more efficient and reproducible code.

  • Advanced bioinformatics: gaining more experience with complex biological

I would particularly like to learn Python and machine learning because these skills could help me analyse larger and more complex datasets in the future.
